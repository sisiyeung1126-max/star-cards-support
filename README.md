# Star Cards support

Static privacy and support pages for the Star Cards iOS app (`app.yuebun.sikzi`).

The pages do not set cookies, and they do not load third-party fonts, scripts, or analytics.

- [Home](index.html)
- [Privacy policy](privacy.html)
- [Support](support.html)

Replace the `SUPPORT_EMAIL` line on the privacy and support pages with a real address before relying on email. No personal email is published in this repository.
